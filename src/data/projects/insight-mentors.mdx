# Insight Mentors

## Overview

Insight Mentors helps students find the right college: search colleges, check official cutoffs and fees, see where they stand, and book counselling.

## What I built

- **College predictor and comparison tool** for students.
- **Scraping pipelines** that ingested **32,000+ admission cutoff records** for **46 colleges and 290+ branches** into a normalized PostgreSQL/Prisma schema.
- A **typed Express.js REST API** serving the student site and the admin panel.
- An **admin CRM dashboard** for lead management and content editing.
- Deployment on Linux behind **Nginx with TLS**.
